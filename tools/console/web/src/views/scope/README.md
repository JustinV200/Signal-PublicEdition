# views/scope

The landing view: a radar scope and a readout of the live state.

- `blips.ts` turns the store state into blips, each a ui mark shape with a label, in its subsystem's sector and opening that subsystem's view: Claude at the centre (a disc while its session is linked, a ring worded `no session` or `session closed` when it isn't, pulsing while busy and slowly while only its agents run); Voice and the board, each a hollow ring while off, at the time since they last did something; pending requests as diamonds beside their subsystem, pulsing. `claudeWords` (the turn words, frozen at the drop while offline), `boardWords` (`off` while the whiteboard is) and `actionView` are shared with the readout. `taskBlips.ts` adds the tasks. One that isn't waiting on the owner is a half disc at its progress (rim 0%, just outside Claude's mark at 100%), labelled with its name and percent; they spread over the left half but off the 30° band either side of 270°, level with Claude. The ones that need the owner are not drawn one by one, since the readout says what each needs: together they are one triangle with a bold label saying how many, `19 NEED YOU` (`1 NEEDS YOU` for one, no triangle when none waits), fixed on that 270° line at 0.9 of the radius, its label running in towards the centre. The band keeps every ordinary mark off that line (the nearest one, a nearly finished task beside the centre, stays 23 units past the end of a three-digit count), everything else on the face is on the right half or at the centre, and Claude's label is one line lower; so the count's label is pinned and always shows whole.
- `needsYou.ts` splits the waiting tasks at a cap (`NEEDS_YOU_SHOWN`, 3) into the ones the readout shows and a folded count. The radar's count comes from the same split, so the two always agree.
- `readout.ts` turns it into the readout rows, what needs the owner first: `NEEDS YOU` (the `you` text of each of the first three waiting tasks, then `+16 more` for the folded rest, or `nothing`), `CHAT`, `CLAUDE`, `VOICE`, `BOARD`, `YOU`, and `RUNNING` while a button's request runs; each row names the view it opens, and every value ends with `(last known)` while the server isn't answering.
- `Scope.tsx` draws one SVG (400 units at 400px, so text never scales) from `radar/`: `Grid` (the falloff face, rings, crosshair, ticks, sector edges), `Sweep` (one turn per 5 s server keepalive), `RadarText` (every line of text, `--font-small` with a halo and a plate), `Blips` (labels, then marks on top; only marks glow and pulse; a click opens the view), `labels.ts` (places each label towards the centre or away, cut to fit, hiding the lower-priority ones on a crowded face; the two pinned labels, Claude's and the needs-you count's, are not fitted around the rest: each always shows whole in its own spot), `captions.ts` (ring captions `now / 1m / 5m`, sector names, the `NO SIGNAL 25s` banner), `scale.ts` (sectors, percent and age to range, `signalWords`) and `geometry.ts`.
- `ScopeView` lays the two out, each readout row a button that opens its view, and stops the sweep while the page is hidden or the stream is stale or down (`NO SIGNAL`); reduced motion stops it and the pulses too.

Public API: `index.ts` (`ScopeView`, and `buildBlips`, `signalWords`, `buildReadout` with `LAST_KNOWN` for tests). Depends on `api` (task types, `isStreamStale`), `state` (chat count, pending rows, board words), `status` (phase, turn, away and new-count words), `time` (`shortAge`), `ui` (marks, page visibility).
