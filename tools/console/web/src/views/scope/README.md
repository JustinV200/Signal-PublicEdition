# views/scope

The landing view: a radar scope and a readout of the live state.

- `blips.ts` turns the store state into blips, each a ui mark shape with a label, in its subsystem's sector and opening that subsystem's view: Claude at the centre (a disc while its session is linked, a ring worded `no session` or `session closed` when it isn't, pulsing while busy and slowly while only its agents run); Voice and the board, each a hollow ring while off, at the time since they last did something; pending requests as diamonds beside their subsystem, pulsing. `claudeWords` (the turn words, frozen at the drop while offline), `boardWords` (`off` while the whiteboard is) and `actionView` are shared with the readout. `taskBlips.ts` adds one blip per active task at its progress (rim 0%, just outside Claude's mark at 100%), spread over the left half but off the 30° band either side of 270°, level with Claude: a half disc, or a triangle with a bold, never-hidden label for one that needs the owner; the label is the name and percent.
- `readout.ts` turns it into the readout rows, what needs the owner first: `NEEDS YOU` (each waiting task's `you` text, or `nothing`), `CHAT`, `CLAUDE`, `VOICE`, `BOARD`, `YOU`, and `RUNNING` while a button's request runs; each row names the view it opens, and every value ends with `(last known)` while the server isn't answering.
- `Scope.tsx` draws one SVG (400 units at 400px, so text never scales) from `radar/`: `Grid` (the falloff face, rings, crosshair, ticks, sector edges), `Sweep` (one turn per 5 s server keepalive), `RadarText` (every line of text, `--font-small` with a halo and a plate), `Blips` (labels, then marks on top; only marks glow and pulse; a click opens the view), `labels.ts` (places each label towards the centre or away, cut to fit, hiding the lower-priority ones on a crowded face; Claude's and needs-you labels never hide), `captions.ts` (ring captions `now / 1m / 5m`, sector names, the `NO SIGNAL 25s` banner), `scale.ts` (sectors, percent and age to range, `signalWords`) and `geometry.ts`.
- `ScopeView` lays the two out, each readout row a button that opens its view, and stops the sweep while the page is hidden or the stream is stale or down (`NO SIGNAL`); reduced motion stops it and the pulses too.

Public API: `index.ts` (`ScopeView`, and `buildBlips`, `signalWords`, `buildReadout` with `LAST_KNOWN` for tests). Depends on `api` (task types, `isStreamStale`), `state` (chat count, pending rows, board words), `status` (phase, turn, away and new-count words), `time` (`shortAge`), `ui` (marks, page visibility).
