# help

`HelpPanel` is the block that opens from the status line's Help button (`helpOpen` in the store). The shell renders it, with `TourBar`, between the status line and the view, outside the view's scroller, so it shows at once however far the view is scrolled; past `60vh` it scrolls inside its own box. Three short blocks: what each tab is for (`VIEW_HELP`, with what the views' old standing hints said), with `Take the tour` on its heading row; the keys (y / n among them, and Esc, which closes Commands, a question or this help but never changes view; the tabs carry theirs only as tooltips); and "If something is wrong": start at System (`Open System`), Refresh, then `ui`'s `OpenCommand` (the `console open` line with Copy). The status line's `Close help` is its one close button; Esc closes it too. `Open System` closes Help, then opens System (a paused tour follows it there, System being one of its stops). On a short window Help shrinks inside the shell's band and scrolls in its own box. Help and the tour show one at a time: the store's `tour` action closes Help, and `TourBar` hides while Help is open, keeping its step. It is the shared `.panel` on the frame, kept to the 96ch reading column, its terms in the shared label column so the words line up with Chat's text.

`VIEW_HELP` is the one source of each tab's text: `TourBar`, the console tour (started by the setup wizard's Tour screen or `Take the tour`), shows the same words one view at a time, switching to it, with Back, Next and End tour (`tourStop` in the store), on a `.panel` with an accent rule on its left. Its stops and their order come from `state`'s `tourStops`: Chat (whose text says how to link Claude, Commands and Hand to Claude), Tasks, System, Scope, then Voice and Boards only when audiohub and the whiteboard are picked. A tab click moves the tour to that tab's stop (the store's `view` action); a tab that isn't a stop reads `TOUR · <tab>` with where Back and Next go. It starts at the last stop it reached (`resumeTourStop`, kept in `localStorage` by `saveTourStop`; Finish forgets it, End tour keeps it). The label and buttons sit in two grid columns (one column, buttons under the label, below 720px), Next is as wide as Finish and Back's `first stop` is a tooltip, so Back, Next and End tour never move between stops. A stop's words scroll in their own box past 30vh, so on a short window part of the tab still shows below the bar.

Public API: `index.ts`. Depends on `state` and `ui`.
